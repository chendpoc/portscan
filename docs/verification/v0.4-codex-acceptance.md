## Remaining acceptance limits

Terminal launch was exercised and fixed `/usr/bin/open -a Terminal <directory>` arguments/refusal paths passed tests, but the opened Terminal window CWD could not be inspected: the computer-use tool disallows `com.apple.Terminal`. No bypass was used. Native permission-denied and unknown-owner cases were not observed in the final probe (222CWDs were explicitly unavailable); unit/mock evidence is separate. Full-cycle sustained performance, signing, notarization and Intel hardware acceptance are not claimed.
